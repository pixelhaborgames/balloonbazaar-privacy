Balloon Bazaar: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Balloon Bazaar is a mobile game published by Nova Forge Games. This page explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect or store any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

We store no personal data, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the game removes all saved progress from your device.
